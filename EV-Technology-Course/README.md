# EV Technology Course

A self-study course on electric-vehicle technology beyond the battery: vehicle architecture, each major component and how it works, control, testing, manufacturing and cost, and new technologies. It follows the EV Battery Engineering Course in this repo. Each PDF uses the same lesson format: objectives, theory, diagram, equations, worked examples, case study, revision notes, glossary, questions and answer key.

Prepared October 2026. Market and policy figures in Modules 14, 15 and 16 carry their source dates; cost figures in Modules 13 and 17 are illustrative teaching assumptions.
