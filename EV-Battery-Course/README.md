# EV Battery Engineering Course

A self-study course on electric-vehicle battery technology, from fundamentals to advanced engineering. Each PDF follows the same lesson format: objectives, theory, equations, worked examples, case study, revision notes, glossary, questions and answer key.

Prepared October 2026. Market and policy figures in Modules 4, 14 and 15 carry their source dates.
